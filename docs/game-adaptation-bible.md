# Game Adaptation Bible

## Purpose

This document translates the opening arc of `末世灾厄-都市篇` into game canon. It is not a rewrite of the manuscript. It defines the playable chronology, character baseline, adaptation boundaries, stage structure, and first vertical slice.

## Canon Rules

- Linxi is 18 at the beginning of the game adaptation.
- The original manuscript remains unchanged as source material.
- The game is a single-player, fake-3D narrative action brawler built around exploration, combat, transformation, and return-to-hub reflection.
- The shelter/safe-house page is a mouse-driven between-stage interface and narrative space, not a walkable gameplay map. Crossing the school main gate commits Linxi's body/story state and opens this UI directly; the shelter restores HP while preserving biomass, weight, capacity, contained prey, body-load regions, and partial digestion progress for inspection.
- The memory settings menu is the pause menu. It supports window size, volume, key binding review, resume, and exit game; it does not commit progression.
- The current Evolution Room is a non-canon training simulation and replay encounter accessible from the safe house.
- Graphic or sexual material from the fiction is adapted only when it serves character, horror, or gameplay. It must never undermine Linxi's adult characterization or turn major emotional events into spectacle.
- The opening self-harm crisis is treated seriously, without scoring, rewards, comedy, or interactive reenactment of the act.

## Core Fantasy

The player begins as an isolated young adult with little physical confidence and limited control over her life. A citywide biochemical attack changes her into an intelligent infected being. Survival initially depends on caution, observation, and improvised action. As Linxi learns how her body works, combat becomes faster and more expressive, but every increase in power creates questions about appetite, identity, restraint, and how other survivors see her.

The intended progression is:

`vulnerable human -> disoriented infected survivor -> capable T-form fighter -> biomass specialist -> temporary G-form powerhouse`

## Design Pillars

- Responsiveness before content volume.
- Strong silhouettes and readable effects.
- Data-driven combat, stage, level, enemy, and item tuning.
- Original characters and visual identity.
- Small systems that can grow after the demo proves enjoyable.
- Five-to-ten minute vertical slices are preferred over broad unfinished content.

## Core Loop

Enter a stage level, explore the fake-3D combat belt, read the story situation, position against enemies, use body/temporary weapons, survive the authored encounter, commit progress at a valid shelter or safe-house checkpoint, and advance to the next level.

The game should not become a full open world. Its best structure is a linear plot spine with route-map freedom layered on top: the story unlocks scenes in authored order, then shelters and safe houses let the player revisit completed scenes, choose among currently unlocked routes, review archives, and prepare equipment. This gives the player agency without breaking pacing, tutorial order, or permanent-growth balance.

## Opening-Arc Source Timeline

1. Linxi is a socially isolated student living away from her family in W City. Su Ruo is her closest roommate and emotional connection.
2. During a severe personal crisis, Linxi falls from the school building and loses consciousness.
3. In parallel, an underground institute conducts virus experiments. Subject 47 appears to die, an infiltrator compromises containment, and the facility begins to collapse.
4. Chopper-dropped nebulizers and aerosol devices are deployed near the school, central hospital, and Black King nightclub, creating several outbreak centers.
5. Linxi regains consciousness with catastrophic injuries but no normal pain response. Her body begins repairing itself.
6. She discovers and consumes a blue experimental substance. Her vision, healing, hunger, and physiology change rapidly.
7. The school outbreak begins while most students are in their dormitories. Linxi remains conscious but develops predatory instincts and an altered relationship with ordinary infected.
8. Her first feeding produces visible biomass adaptation. Bone-blade presentation belongs to a later progression beat in the game adaptation rather than the current Red Night demo.
9. Linxi returns to the dormitory, changes clothes, and searches for Su Ruo. Su Ruo is absent, suggesting she escaped during the initial chaos.
10. Linxi crosses the school grounds, confronts infected and human threats, follows evidence of Su Ruo's route into the teaching building, meets hidden classroom survivors, and eventually leaves campus for the wider city.
11. The city broadcasts emergency instructions. The school, hospital, and nightclub are identified as major contamination zones.
12. Linxi marks future destinations, including a shopping center and hospital, establishing the broader urban campaign.

## Game-Canon Linxi

### Starting Profile

- Name: Linxi
- Age: 18
- Height before transformation: approximately 165 cm
- Home: S City
- Current location: residential school in W City
- Closest known relationship: Su Ruo, roommate and trusted friend
- Initial strengths: observant, imaginative, stubborn, capable of adapting under pressure
- Initial weaknesses: isolated, physically inexperienced, emotionally overwhelmed, uncertain whether she deserves help

### Human Form

- Appears in the opening cinematic, conceptual art, memories, portraits, and the safe house's symbolic presentation.
- Human Linxi is not a normal playable story-stage form after the fall.
- She represents Linxi's inner identity: despite infected clothing, altered biology, and later forms, she still understands herself as the same young woman.
- Human Linxi establishes relationships, environment, and the emotional stakes of losing her former life.

### Early T-Form

- Linxi enters Early T-form when the virus revives and repairs her after the fall. This is her first playable story-stage form.
- Human proportions and consciousness are retained.
- Pale skin, altered eyes, accelerated healing, reduced pain response, abnormal hunger, and infected senses appear first.
- Early combat begins tactical and awkward.
- Claws are the current first playable body weapon after the blue substance. Wrist bone blade remains a later fiction-grounded innate weapon.
- Feeding and digestion unlock biomass growth, but the player must learn these systems gradually rather than receiving the full prototype kit immediately.

### Developed T-Form

- Faster combo flow and confident lane movement.
- Improved control over hunger, healing, armor growth, and biomass spending.
- Intake routes unlock through story events and biological experimentation.
- Linxi remains visually recognizable and able to communicate with survivors.

### G-Form

- A temporary, approximately three-meter combat transformation.
- Biomass exoskeleton, fixed heavy movement, high durability, and expanded capacity.
- Introduced substantially later than the school opening.
- It should feel frightening and costly, not like an ordinary cooldown skill.

## Principal Opening Characters

### Su Ruo

- Linxi's roommate and closest friend.
- Her absence drives the school arc.
- The player follows traces of her escape rather than immediately finding her.
- Relationship themes: trust, fear of abandonment, and whether Su Ruo can accept transformed Linxi.

### Subject 47

- A victim of the institute's human experimentation.
- Establishes that the outbreak is engineered and that infected outcomes vary.
- Best introduced through a short non-playable prologue, surveillance archive, or later recovered footage.

### The Infiltrator

- Sabotages the institute and helps trigger the wider incident.
- His identity and organization remain a mystery during the opening stages.
- Functions as a campaign-level antagonist thread rather than an immediate boss.

### Institute Director

- Represents the official research program and concealed knowledge surrounding the virus.
- May become an uneasy source of information later.

## Opening Locations

### School Rooftop and Courtyard

- Emotional prologue and first post-transformation awakening.
- Establishes vertical landmarks, the dropped chopper nebulizer, and the contrast between familiar school architecture and biological disaster.

### Dormitory

- First exploration space.
- Contains Linxi's room, Su Ruo's empty bed, clothing, personal records, and evidence of the outbreak.
- Supports environmental storytelling and the first meaningful choice about approaching survivors or infected.

### Teaching Building

- First full combat stage.
- Dense infected presence, locked stairwell routes, frightened survivors, and evidence of Su Ruo's movement.
- Introduces lane combat, crowd avoidance, and alternate routes.

### School Gate

- Opening-arc climax and transition to the city map.
- Linxi discovers evidence that Su Ruo escaped.
- The emergency broadcast reveals the scale of the W City incident.

### Underground Institute

- Initially shown only through archives, intercut scenes, or recovered footage.
- Becomes a later playable location after the player understands the outbreak's immediate effects.

## Campaign Structure From the Opening Arc

The full 20+ hour campaign structure lives in `campaign-stage-plan.md`. This section defines the opening arc that should become the first polished vertical slice.

### Prologue: Before the Fall

- Human Linxi in the dormitory and school.
- Short conversations establish Su Ruo and school pressure.
- Ends with a non-interactive, carefully framed crisis transition and loss of consciousness.

### Stage 1: Red Night

Stage goal: Linxi finds out the world has changed. Player goal: learn how to control Linxi, interact with items, read objectives, fight basic enemies, and enter the shelter/save rhythm. The opening should not feel like a normal combat mission first. It should move from bodily confusion, to dormitory horror, to survivor collapse, to the realization that the whole city is part of the incident.

#### Courtyard Fall Site

- Linxi wakes in the courtyard during the outbreak, but Red Night should become the full first opening route rather than only the courtyard slice.
- Tutorial: standing up from collapse, weak-form movement, inspection, altered senses, healing, claw combat, route transitions, human-student rescue/consumption choice, dorm investigation, and first elite pressure.
- Controls taught: WASD movement, fake-3D depth movement, camera follow, interactable highlight, F interaction, J claw attack, dodge, Vore against valid targets, and route-circle confirmation.
- Objective: understand why Linxi is alive, why her body feels wrong, why the first infected student does not behave like a normal enemy, whether Linxi can still choose to protect people, and how to leave the school.
- Transformation milestone: consume the blue experimental substance, unlock claws, and receive the first biological achievement: `what's inside the vial`.
- Target beat order: Falling, awake, drink virus solution, weak again, claw fight, save or consume human students, enter dormitory lobby, climb to the scrolling second-floor hallway, find Su Ruo's room, dorm fight, daytime transition, upstairs route, roof fight, return halfway toward the teaching building across the playground, elite bone-blade zombie, teaching-building classroom survivors, then outside the school.
- Current implementation note: the courtyard is a two-plate stitched map using `red_night_courtyard_visual_data.tres`; route and encounter coordinates should be authored against the current `3280` map length rather than older blockout values. The map length is tied to the approved background's rendered scroll span.

#### Dormitory Lobby And Second Floor

- The dormitory is a two-layer route.
- First floor: `Dormitory Lobby`, `FIXED_ROOM`, uses the generated switchback-staircase lobby background. It should feel like a compact interior room with no camera scroll.
- Second floor: `Dormitory Second Floor`, `SCROLLING`, uses the generated long hallway background. It should feel like a corridor with multiple doors and room-route hooks.
- Second-floor art note: the current accepted plate pair is hallway v3 plus stair-side v4. The v4 plate was regenerated from the first hallway plate as reference to preserve corridor continuity.
- The courtyard gate transfers to the lobby, not directly to Su Ruo's room.
- The lobby staircase transfers to the second-floor hallway; Su Ruo's room is reached from the hallway door.

#### Dormitory: Su Ruo's Room

- Explore the dormitory and Linxi's room.
- Find Su Ruo missing and collect the first relationship archive.
- Tutorial: cautious interaction with infected, first feeding, digestion, and the first visible body-state consequences.
- Controls taught: objective reading, item inspection, archive pickup, dialogue/window reading, and safe movement around non-hostile infected.
- Objective: understand that the dormitory changed while Linxi was unconscious, then follow Su Ruo's route toward the teaching building.

#### Roof Route

- First complete fake-3D brawler stage.
- Tactical early encounters evolve into smoother combat as Linxi adapts.
- Enemy groups: ordinary infected, panicked humans, and one mutated infected elite.
- Controls taught: J attack, dodge timing, enemy range, stun, knockdown, temporary weapon use, and first safe feeding/digestion window.
- Objective: learn that the world changed socially as well as biologically, then locate evidence of Su Ruo.

#### Teaching Building Lobby And Second Floor

- Reconnects the playable flow to the manuscript's teaching-building survivor route.
- The twins are adapted as the conversation beat that confirms Su Ruo's trail points beyond the school gate and that the W City disaster is larger than the school.
- The current resource slot is `red_night_teaching_building`; final classroom background art still needs to be generated through the map pipeline.

#### Outside The School

- School-gate confrontation and opening-arc boss or pursuit sequence.
- Recover Su Ruo's personal clue.
- Receive the emergency broadcast naming the city's outbreak centers.
- Controls taught: level objective completion, route/map reading, shelter entry, progress commit, and archive review.
- Objective: realize the school is only one outbreak point in a wider city disaster.
- Unlock the W City mission map and the first true safe house.

## First Vertical Slice

The first production slice should combine Stage 1 and the beginning of Stage 2 into 15-20 minutes.

### Playable Flow

1. Brief human-form dormitory conversation with Su Ruo.
2. Elliptical crisis transition; no interactive self-harm sequence.
3. Linxi wakes in the courtyard after the outbreak.
4. Player learns ground movement, depth movement, interaction, and environmental inspection.
5. Linxi wakes on the ground with the school building not far behind the fall site, then pushes herself upright with her arms before normal control begins.
6. Linxi reaches the chopper-dropped nebulizer and drinks the blue virus stock solution.
7. Linxi loses consciousness again; the screen falls to black and cuts to a brief school/classroom chase scene where infected pursue fleeing students.
8. Player control returns to weak-form Linxi after the chase cutscene. The first zombie student wanders without attacking, teaching that ordinary infected do not initially treat Linxi as normal prey because she is infected too.
9. Linxi finishes the blue-vial drink beat, claws unlock from inside her body, and the `what's inside the vial` achievement appears.
10. Linxi completes the first controlled claw fight.
11. Human students become the first moral pressure point: Linxi can try to save them or consume them.
12. Player enters the dormitory and finds Su Ruo's room.
13. Dormitory fight escalates the level from feeding tutorial into survival pressure.
14. Daytime/upstairs/roof sequence reframes the school from a single courtyard to a larger collapsed route.
15. Linxi returns halfway across the playground toward the teaching building.
16. Elite bone-blade zombie encounter guards the teaching-building approach.
17. Linxi enters the teaching-building lobby, climbs to the second-floor survivor classroom, and meets the twins.
18. Linxi exits the school, unlocking the next route/shelter flow.

### Mechanics Included

- Fake-3D movement and camera
- Mouse interaction only in the safe house; keyboard interaction in story stages
- Opening cinematic Human identity followed by playable Early T-form; no freely selectable Human/T-form switch
- Basic body-attack combo, currently implemented as claw/control attacks while wrist bone blade presentation remains a later art and story refinement
- Enemy approach, attack, stun, and knockdown
- One simple digestion tutorial presented as survival horror, not a full progression farm
- Dialogue window and character avatar
- Archive unlocks
- Checkpoint and return-to-hub flow

### Mechanics Deferred

- Story introduction of G mode. The prototype key/mechanics may exist for testing, but Red Night should not present G-form as a normal opening-stage power.
- Full six-route intake presentation. The prototype route names may exist in code, but the playable Red Night scope is belly-first and should not rely on disabled route visuals.
- Large simultaneous capacity
- Complex weapon inventory
- Multiple enemy families beyond humans and ordinary infected
- Open-city travel
- Relationship branching
- Permanent maximum-HP farming

## Shelter And Safe House Role

The shelter/safe-house page represents Linxi organizing memory, equipment, and allies between dangerous sorties. It is visually warmer and safer than the fallen city: amber light, cream surfaces, muted wood, soft green, and Human Linxi imagery. This contrast expresses that Linxi remains human on the inside even when her mission-stage body is T-zombie or G-form. The current mouse-driven hub remains appropriate, but its opening-story presentation should be framed as one of the following:

1. A later survivor shelter, with early stages accessed as memories and records.
2. A non-literal preparation interface layered over Linxi's consciousness.
3. A temporary room reached only after the school arc.

The recommended canon is option 3. Before Stage 4, use lightweight checkpoint and archive screens rather than implying Linxi already owns a secure base.

## Shelter And Save Role

The game should separate three similar-looking but mechanically different spaces:

- **Memory Settings:** pause/settings menu opened during gameplay. It pauses simulation and allows window-size adjustment, master volume adjustment, key binding review, resume, and exit game. It is not a save point and does not show progression pages.
- **Shelter Page:** a local safe room after an encounter or authored checkpoint. It can be a classroom office, storage room, infirmary corner, rooftop access room, or other believable temporary refuge. This is where the game may commit player/story progression inside a stage and provide mission board, archive terminal, training, status, equipment, and character pages when appropriate.
- **Safe House:** a larger between-stage hub that appears once the plot supports it. It uses the same page functions as shelters, but with stronger narrative presence, more conversations, and broader mission selection.

Confirmed map switches save the route checkpoint and allowed Linxi/story progression, but they do not make enemy HP, positions, cast timers, or repeatable encounter depletion permanent. This lets the player move between authored maps without losing real progress while still preventing the old "swallow one enemy, exit, refresh the stage" exploit.

Shelters and the true safe house are the stage-gate saves. Before confirming the next stage, the player may return to currently unlocked maps and reclaim missed route content. After confirming the next stage, there is no normal return to the previous stage except explicit replay/archive modes. The memory settings menu only pauses and changes settings; it must not become a way to inspect-and-commit active combat rewards.

For Red Night, the first shelter should appear only after an authored encounter is complete, not before the first zombie student beat. The shelter can become the transition space where Linxi catches her breath, reviews what changed in her body, and then moves into the next encounter.

## Prototype Reconciliation

- Evolution Room: retained as safe-house training simulation, not a literal pre-incident event.
- Ten human guards: retained for combat testing and replay scoring.
- Service handgun: retained as a training/loadout weapon; story acquisition occurs later.
- Weight and digestion: retained, but introduced gradually during Stage 2.
- Evolution after six hits: retained as a training abstraction; story stages unlock fluid combat through authored milestones.
- G mode: remains a late-game prototype toggle until its narrative introduction is designed.
